## V4B CATEGORY INSIGHTS (₹1L-deal validated)

### SKINCARE POSITIONING LADDER
| Tier | Price | Product Shots | Actor Shots | Example | Pacing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Luxury** | ₹1000+ | 0% (none) | 100% lifestyle + portraits | SunScreen (Dot & Key) | 2.0s avg |
| **Mid-tier** | ₹500-1000 | 60% product+usage | 40% lifestyle | Night Cream | 2.45s avg |
| **Mass** | ₹200-500 | 80% product hero | 20% actor context | (not in V4B portfolio) | 2.0s avg |

**Rule:** Luxury sells FEELING (glowing skin, outdoor confidence), not OBJECT (cream jar). Position brand first, then decide product visibility %.

### AUTOMOTIVE DUAL PLAYBOOK
| Approach | Void Color | Environment | Text Focus | Pacing | Target Buyer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TECH/SPEC** | White studio | Minimal (turntable) | Specs (range, 0-60, autopilot) | 3.0s slow | Logic-first (EV, tech) |

**Models:** Tesla = tech playbook. BMW/Mercedes/luxury ICE = prestige playbook.

### HEALTH/NUTRITION STRUCTURE
1. **Material truth open** (3-5s): Powder/texture macro, no text, warm lighting
2. **Triple-panel lifestyle** (15-20s): 1 face across 3 contexts (gym, outdoor, office), rapid cuts 1.5-2s
3. **Product PNG composite** (5-8s): Real bottle on solid category color (green for health), benefit stack text
4. **Brand CTA** (2-4s): Logo + tagline

**Cost:** ~$0.30 per 30s ad. **Time:** 4-5 hours. **Efficiency:** 1 face → 3 scenes = 3x variety for 1x gen cost.

### FMCG INGREDIENT SURROUND
**When:** Food/bev where raw ingredients = the story (protein bars, supplements, snacks)  
**Setup:** Hero product centered, surrounded by ingredient bowls (chocolate, almonds, peanut butter) on warm wooden surface  
**Why:** Cheaper than actors, more authentic than stock, tells flavor story without VO  
**Technique:** Real product + real bowls shot together OR separate + composite, golden hour 4500-5000K  
**Example:** MyFitness bar (5.5s hero hold with ingredient surround = confidence pacing)

### NARRATIVE/BRAND FILMS (60s+)
| Type | Avg Shot | Structure | V4B Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CSR/cause** | 4.4s | VO-driven, multiple actors, brand at 85-95% | DBS Women's Day (89s) |
| **Testimonial** | 6.0s | Emotional arc, slow pacing, trust building | Living Memories (120s) |
| **Tribute** | 10s+ (0 cuts) | Crossfade transitions, reverence | Tribute to Doctors (75s, zero cuts) |

**Rule:** Slower cuts = deeper emotion. Product ads = 2s, emotion films = 6s. Use crossfades (not hard cuts) for healthcare/tribute subjects.
