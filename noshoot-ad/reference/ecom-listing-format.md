# eCOM / D2C LISTING VIDEO — the conversion spine
Biggest slice of v4b's catalog ("marketplace-optimized", "boost add-to-cart"). NOT a cinematic
ad — a listing video engineered to convert on Amazon/Flipkart/PDP.

## SHAPE
15-30s · loops cleanly · works MUTED (autoplay is silent → every claim = on-screen text) ·
9:16 or 1:1 primary, 4:5 feed, 16:9 only if platform needs.

## SPINE
1. HOOK (0-2s): product doing its one best thing, or the problem. Big, centered. Viewer scrolls in 1s.
2. PRODUCT TRUTH (2-8s): clean hero turntable/orbit. Label MUST be legible (3D model or locked
   plate; text-on-product = #1 AI tell).
3. FEATURE/BENEFIT CALLOUTS (8-20s): 2-4 punchy on-screen callouts, one per micro-beat,
   benefit first. Macro texture shots prove quality.
4. PROOF (optional): rating, bestseller, certification, ingredient close-up.
5. CTA/PACKSHOT (last 2-4s): product + logo + add-to-cart energy, brand-color bg.

## RULES
- Text IS the product (muted autoplay). Legible, high-contrast, safe-area aware.
- One idea per beat. Hero angle held — buyer must recognize what ships.
- Turntable = infinite clean listing content ("one accurate 3D model → infinite content").
- Real scale: show with a hand/context (AI gets product-vs-hand scale wrong constantly).

## HANDOFF PER VIDEO
Hook plate + motion · callout copy (benefit-led) + AE text styling · packshot/CTA frame ·
QC: failure-modes-qc.md + text legible at mobile size + muted-readable + safe-area + loop seam.

## CINEMATIC vs LISTING (don't mix)
Cinematic: brand feeling · sensory sparse copy · music-led · product can be atmospheric ·
15-60s · cut on musical beats.
Listing: add-to-cart · benefit callouts on-screen · must work muted · product legible,
hero-locked, real scale · 15-30s loopable · cut on information beats.
