# PROMPT STRUCTURE FORMULA — Validated Cinema-Grade Templates

**Source:** AiKAMI Day 2 Vocabulary PDF pages 6-8 (2026-08-18 integration)

This document dissects 5 validated prompts to show HOW to combine the camera vocabulary 
layer with scene detail. Use these as templates for your own prompts.

---

## THE FORMULA (5-Layer Structure)

Every cinema-grade image prompt follows this structure:

```
LAYER 1: CAMERA + LENS + DIRECTOR STYLE
Shot on [CAMERA], [LENS], in the style of [DIRECTOR], [FILM REFERENCE] vibe.

LAYER 2: SHOT TYPE + SUBJECT + SPATIAL RELATIONSHIP
[Shot size + angle] of [subject exact description], [spatial position in frame].

LAYER 3: TECHNICAL DETAILS (Scale, Depth, Atmosphere)
[Negative space, layering, depth cues, atmosphere, environmental context].

LAYER 4: LIGHTING + MOOD
[Key light direction + type], [fill/rim/practicals], [mood adjectives].

LAYER 5: COLOR + TEXTURE + FINISH
Palette: [hex codes]. [Texture beats]. Photoreal, [camera] + [lens], [resolution].
```

**Critical:** Layers 1-5 work TOGETHER. Layer 1 controls the aesthetic signature; 
Layers 2-5 execute the detailed scene. Never skip Layer 1 (defaults to generic AI look).

## EXAMPLE 1: FASHION EDITORIAL (Moody Narrative)

### Full Prompt
```
Shot on Kodak Portra 400, 35mm film, in the style of Wong Kar-wai, 
"In the Mood for Love" (2000) vibe.

Medium shot of a woman in a flowing red silk dress walking through a dimly lit 
alleyway at night, neon signs glowing in the background casting pink and blue 
light across her face. She walks slowly, neither aware of the camera nor 
looking directly at it, natural candid movement. The alley walls are wet from 
recent rain, reflecting neon streaks. Camera follows her from behind at a 
slight distance as she moves deeper into the frame.

Shallow depth of field, background neon signs dissolving to soft bokeh. 
Foreground wet pavement in soft focus framing the lower third. Mood: melancholic, 
romantic, urban loneliness.

Lighting: practical neon signs only (pink, blue, cyan), no studio lights, deep 
shadows between pools of colored light. Natural falloff, high contrast.

Palette: deep black shadows #0A0A0C, neon pink #FF006E, electric blue #00D9FF, 
warm amber reflections #FFA500. Texture: wet pavement sheen, silk fabric flow, 
film grain visible throughout.

Photoreal, 35mm Kodak Portra 400 film stock, organic grain, 4K scan.
```

### Layer-by-Layer Breakdown

**LAYER 1 (Camera/Lens/Director):**
- **Camera:** Kodak Portra 400 (warm film stock, organic grain, nostalgic)
- **Lens:** 35mm film (slightly wider than normal, environmental context)
- **Director:** Wong Kar-wai (saturated neon moody romantic)
- **Film Reference:** "In the Mood for Love" (specific color/mood palette)
- **Why:** Fashion + urban night + emotional narrative = Wong Kar-wai signature

**LAYER 2 (Shot Type + Subject):**
- "Medium shot of a woman..." (torso to head, not full body)
- "in a flowing red silk dress" (material + color specified)
- "walking through a dimly lit alleyway" (action + environment)
- "neither aware of camera nor looking directly" (candid, not posed)

**LAYER 3 (Technical Details):**
- "Shallow depth of field, background neon signs dissolving to soft bokeh"
- "Foreground wet pavement in soft focus framing lower third" (layering)
- "Alley walls wet from rain, reflecting neon streaks" (atmosphere)
- "Camera follows from behind at slight distance" (movement)

**LAYER 4 (Lighting + Mood):**
- "Practical neon signs only (pink, blue, cyan), no studio lights"
- "Deep shadows between pools of colored light"
- "Natural falloff, high contrast"
- Mood: "melancholic, romantic, urban loneliness"

**LAYER 5 (Color + Texture):**
- Palette: 4 hex codes (black, pink, blue, amber)
- Texture: "wet pavement sheen, silk fabric flow, film grain"
- Finish: "35mm Kodak Portra 400 film stock, organic grain, 4K scan"

**What makes this work:** Layer 1 sets the Wong Kar-wai aesthetic (neon moody romantic). 
Layers 2-5 execute that aesthetic with specific scene details that MATCH the Wong Kar-wai 
signature (neon signs, wet reflections, candid movement, melancholic mood).

## EXAMPLE 2: LUXURY PRODUCT (Minimal Epic)

### Full Prompt
```
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, 85mm portrait lens, in the style of Denis Villeneuve, 
"Blade Runner 2049" aesthetic.

Close-up product shot of a sleek black smartwatch on a minimalist concrete 
surface, watch positioned on left third of frame, vast empty negative space 
filling the right two-thirds. Concrete surface is raw board-formed with 
visible wood grain texture, extending into darkness at the edges. Watch face 
glows with subtle blue UI elements.

Epic scale created through negative space and minimal composition. Single 
architectural element (concrete beam) cutting diagonally across upper 
background out of focus. No clutter, no props, absolute restraint.

Dramatic side lighting from left at 30 degrees, creating sharp shadow of 
watch extending right across empty concrete. One subtle blue accent light 
from behind right (motivated by screen glow). Deep controlled shadows, 
background falling to near-black. Mood: cold, precise, futuristic, monumental.

Palette: concrete grey #3A3A3C, absolute black #000000, ice blue UI glow 
#00A8E8, cool steel watch body #1C1C1E. Texture: raw concrete pores and 
grain, brushed metal watch case, glowing screen pixels.

Photoreal, ARRI Alexa 65 large format, Master Prime 85mm, 8K, cinematic grade.
```

### Layer-by-Layer Breakdown

**LAYER 1:**
- **Camera:** ARRI Alexa 65 (large format, epic scale, blockbuster clean)
- **Lens:** 85mm portrait (isolated subject, compressed background)
- **Director:** Denis Villeneuve (vast minimal sci-fi grandeur)
- **Film Reference:** "Blade Runner 2049" (brutalist concrete + neon accents)
- **Why:** Tech product + minimal luxury positioning = Villeneuve cold beauty

**LAYER 2:**
- "Close-up product shot of sleek black smartwatch"
- "on minimalist concrete surface" (material specified)
- "watch positioned on left third, vast empty negative space right two-thirds"
- Concrete detail: "raw board-formed with visible wood grain texture"

**LAYER 3:**
- "Epic scale created through negative space and minimal composition"
- "Single architectural element (concrete beam) cutting diagonally, out of focus"
- "No clutter, no props, absolute restraint" (anti-maximalist)

**LAYER 4:**
- "Dramatic side lighting from left at 30 degrees"
- "Sharp shadow extending right across empty concrete"
- "One subtle blue accent from behind right (motivated by screen glow)"
- Mood: "cold, precise, futuristic, monumental"

**LAYER 5:**
- Palette: 4 hex codes (grey concrete, black void, blue glow, steel)
- Texture: "concrete pores and grain, brushed metal, glowing pixels"
- Finish: "ARRI Alexa 65 large format, Master Prime 85mm, 8K"

**What makes this work:** The Villeneuve reference (Layer 1) sets the minimal brutalist 
epic aesthetic. Then Layer 3 EXECUTES that with "vast empty negative space" and 
"absolute restraint" — the scene description matches the director's signature style.

## EXAMPLE 3: DOCUMENTARY REALISM (People / Lifestyle)

### Full Prompt
```
Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 50mm lens, in the style of Alfonso Cuarón, natural 
documentary lighting.

Wide shot of a middle-class family of four gathered around a small wooden 
dining table in a modest apartment, warm overhead light from a single bare bulb, 
grandmother serving food while children laugh and father reads a newspaper. 
Everyone engaged in natural activity, nobody posing or looking at camera. 
Apartment walls show signs of age — paint slightly faded, family photos in 
mismatched frames, simple curtains partially open showing evening light outside.

Camera observes from doorway as if unseen, documentary distance, no 
intrusion into the intimate moment. Depth layers: doorframe in foreground 
soft focus, family at table in sharp focus midground, kitchen glimpse 
through background doorway out of focus.

Natural lighting only: single overhead bulb (warm tungsten 2700K), soft 
evening window light from left (cool 5000K blue hour), no fill lights, 
authentic shadows under table and in corners. Mood: warmth, authenticity, 
everyday intimacy, quiet joy.

Palette: warm wood tones #8B5A3C, faded yellow walls #E8D5A3, tungsten 
bulb glow #FFB347, cool blue window light #A8C7E0, grandmother's teal sari 
#0D7E83. Texture: worn wood grain on table, cotton fabric, visible wall 
texture, natural skin tones with pores and imperfections.

Photoreal, ARRI Alexa 35 documentary realism, 50mm lens, natural grain, 4K.
```

### Layer-by-Layer Breakdown

**LAYER 1:**
- **Camera:** ARRI Alexa 35 (gritty textured realism, documentary workhorse)
- **Lens:** 50mm (human eye perspective, natural proportions)
- **Director:** Alfonso Cuarón (long unbroken takes, immersive realism)
- **Lighting Note:** "natural documentary lighting" (no film reference needed)
- **Why:** People ad + authenticity + emotional realism = Cuarón humanist approach

**LAYER 2:**
- "Wide shot of middle-class family of four"
- "gathered around small wooden dining table in modest apartment"
- Action: "grandmother serving, children laughing, father reading"
- "Nobody posing or looking at camera" (candid documentary rule)
- Environment: "walls show age, faded paint, mismatched frames, simple curtains"

**LAYER 3:**
- "Camera observes from doorway as if unseen, documentary distance"
- Depth layers: "doorframe foreground soft, family midground sharp, kitchen background out of focus"
- "No intrusion into intimate moment" (observer not participant)

**LAYER 4:**
- "Natural lighting only: single overhead bulb (warm 2700K)"
- "Soft evening window light from left (cool 5000K blue hour)"
- "No fill lights, authentic shadows under table and in corners"
- Mood: "warmth, authenticity, everyday intimacy, quiet joy"

**LAYER 5:**
- Palette: 5 hex codes (wood, yellow walls, warm glow, cool window, teal sari)
- Texture: "worn wood grain, cotton fabric, wall texture, natural skin pores"
- Finish: "ARRI Alexa 35 documentary realism, 50mm, natural grain, 4K"

**What makes this work:** Cuarón = humanist immersive realism. Layer 2 describes 
"natural activity, nobody posing" and Layer 3 says "camera observes from doorway 
as if unseen" — this MATCHES Cuarón's long-take observational style. The lighting 
(Layer 4) is "natural only" with authentic shadows, reinforcing documentary realism.

## EXAMPLE 4: ACTION / KINETIC ENERGY

### Full Prompt
```

arms driving, face intense with effort. Canyon walls rising dramatically in 
background, harsh midday sun creating strong shadows and dust backlight.

Dynamic diagonal composition, runner exiting frame right creating sense of 
unstoppable momentum. Foreground rocks in partial shadow framing lower left. 
Background canyon depth compressed by wide lens perspective.

Hard overhead sun (12 noon), strong rim light on runner's back and shoulders 
from dust backlight, deep shadows under chin and torso, no fill light, high 
contrast punchy grade. Mood: aggressive, kinetic, relentless, powerful.

Palette: burnt orange athletic gear #FF5A1F, desert sand #D4A574, deep 
shadow blacks #1A1A1C, bright dust backlight #FFF8E7, canyon rock rust 
#8B4513. Texture: fabric under tension, sweat sheen on skin, kicked dust 
particles, rough canyon rock.

Photoreal, RED V-Raptor 8K, 24mm wide lens, high shutter speed freeze, 
punchy digital cinema grade.
```

## COMMON MISTAKES (What NOT To Do)

### ❌ MISTAKE 1: Generic Camera Reference
**Bad:** "Professional commercial photography"
**Why:** Doesn't control aesthetic — model picks random stock-photo look
**Fix:** "Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, 85mm portrait, shot by Roger Deakins"

### ❌ MISTAKE 2: Camera Vocabulary Without Scene Detail
**Bad:** "Shot on ARRI Alexa 65. A perfume bottle."
**Why:** Camera vocab alone = thin prompt, model fills gaps randomly
**Fix:** Add Layers 2-5 (full scene description after camera layer)

### ❌ MISTAKE 3: Scene Detail Without Camera Vocabulary
**Bad:** [500-word detailed scene description] "Photoreal, high quality, 8K"
**Why:** Missing aesthetic signature, defaults to generic AI render look
**Fix:** Add Layer 1 camera/lens/director BEFORE the scene description

### ❌ MISTAKE 4: Director Style That Contradicts Scene
**Bad:** "Shot on Kodak Portra 400, Wong Kar-wai style. Clean white studio, 
even lighting, minimalist product on seamless white background."
**Why:** Wong Kar-wai = neon moody romantic, not clean white studio
**Fix:** Match director to scene aesthetic (Wong Kar-wai → neon alley, not white studio)

### ❌ MISTAKE 5: Too Many Film References
**Bad:** "Blade Runner 2049 meets Mad Max meets Grand Budapest Hotel vibe"
**Why:** Conflicting aesthetics (minimal vs kinetic vs pastel), model confused
**Fix:** ONE film reference per prompt, or none if director name is enough

### ❌ MISTAKE 6: Forgetting The Finish Language
**Bad:** [full detailed prompt] "High quality, realistic, detailed"
**Why:** Generic finish = model forgets the camera claim from Layer 1
**Fix:** Finish MUST echo the camera: "ARRI Alexa 65 + Master Prime, 8K" 
(matches the opener)

## VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Before finalizing any image prompt, verify:

**✅ Layer 1 present?** Shot on [camera], [lens], [director style]
**✅ Camera matches product tier?** (Luxury → ARRI/Sony, Kinetic → RED, etc.)
**✅ Director style matches scene mood?** (Villeneuve = minimal, not cluttered)
**✅ Layers 2-5 fully detailed?** (Subject, technical, lighting, color, texture)
**✅ Finish language echoes camera?** ("ARRI Alexa + Master Prime" not "high quality")
**✅ Palette has hex codes?** (Not color names, actual #HEX values)
**✅ Texture beats specific to category?** (Ice cream = drag ridges, not generic "texture")
**✅ No conflicting references?** (One film vibe, not three blended)

If all 8 checks pass → prompt is cinema-grade ready.
