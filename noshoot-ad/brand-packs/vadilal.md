## AD-COPY STRUCTURE (match this shape, Vadilal words)
Cinematic ad = **one short sensory line per flavor/beat**, cut on the beat, then a 4–6 word
payoff tagline. Pattern: `[Flavor] + [one-word mood/time noun]`. Vadilal draft:
> "Rajbhog evenings. / Butterscotch highs. / Vanilla dreams. / Rich Chocolate nights. /
> One more scoop. / Close your eyes. / **Real ice cream. Real joy.**"
Keep it sensory, concrete, no feature-speak in the cinematic cut. (For an eCom listing cut,
switch to benefit callouts + on-screen text per `../reference/ecom-listing-format.md`.)
